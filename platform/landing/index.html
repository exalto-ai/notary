THE BREAKTHROUGH

A transcript is just text. A sealed trace is evidence.

Anyone can type a transcript, edit one, screenshot one. That's why “here's what the AI said” has never counted for anything.

The insight: your session already runs over an encrypted, authenticated connection to the provider. The protocol relays that session through an independent notary that witnesses exactly what crossed the wire without ever being able to read it. Old cryptography, pointed at a new problem.

TRUST MODEL

A trace proves presence, never absence.

A SEALED TRACE PROVES

  • ✓The disclosed bytes traveled an authenticated connection with the named provider.
  • ✓They have not changed since capture.
  • ✓The named notary witnessed them at the stated time.
  • ✓With live capture (coming soon): the timeline as it actually unfolded.

IT CANNOT PROVE

  • ✕That every interaction was captured or disclosed.
  • ✕Who was at the keyboard.
  • ✕That no other AI or device was used.
  • ✕That an output was correct, lawful, or wise.

We put the limits on the homepage because evidence that overclaims isn't evidence. Full trust model →

Exalto Seal is not a notary public. Sealing is not a notarial act. A receipt is cryptographic evidence, not a legal instrument. The ENP specification uses "notary" as a technical role term, in the way public-key infrastructure uses "certificate authority."
